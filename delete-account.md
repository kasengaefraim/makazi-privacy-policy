# Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your account and associated personal data from Makazi Connect, you can request account deletion using the following method:

**Request account deletion**

Send an email to:

**[support](mailto:kasengaefraim@gmail.com)**
 
Use the subject:

**Account Deletion Request**

Please include the username and phone number associated with your account in international format so that we can identify the account.

After verifying the request, we will delete your account and the personal data associated with it, except for information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

For more information about how we collect, use, and protect your information, please see our Privacy Policy.

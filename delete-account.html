Account Deletion

This page explains how users of Makazi Connect can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Makazi Connect account, please send an account deletion request to our support team by email.

Support Email: gwaka94@gmail.com

Phone: +255659515042

In your account deletion request, please provide:

  • Your username
  • The phone number associated with your account

The username and phone number are required to help us identify and verify the account associated with the deletion request.

Processing Time

After receiving and verifying your request, your account will be deleted within 14 business days.

Important: Please make sure that the username and phone number you provide match the information associated with your Makazi Connect account. We may contact you if additional information is required to verify the request.

What Happens After Deletion?

Once your account deletion request has been processed, your account and personal information associated with the account will be deleted in accordance with our data retention practices.

Certain information may be retained where required by law, necessary for security purposes, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or otherwise permitted by applicable regulations.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Makazi Connect collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.
